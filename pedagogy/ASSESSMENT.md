# Assessment and feedback

status: draft

Use exercises/activities.json. Two activities per module follow its teaching, not before it. The first usually checks a misconception; the second asks for a new application or explanation. After the learner responds, compare the reasoning to every rubric point. Give specific feedback and explain why a plausible alternative fails. An A/B response alone is recognition evidence.

Evidence states: unknown (no attempt/revealed answer), needs_support (a misconception), recognized (correct constrained answer), applied (independent new calculation/explanation), transferred (correct use plus boundaries in a changed context). These are instructional observations, not psychometric scores.

Every activity has objective, prompt, rubric, misconception, feedback, reveal fallback and later callback. All numbers are hypothetical. Accept equivalent units and conservative rounding if justified. Ask for clarification when a numerical result hides a critical assumption.

## Final theory gate

After module 16, assess the critical objectives listed in course.json with fresh prompts from exercises/FINAL.md, withholding solutions until attempted. An objective is resolved only when the learner independently meets every rubric point; recognizing a supplied answer or copying a worked example is insufficient. A failed or skipped case stays unresolved. Teach the gap and offer a different case on a later turn; there is no fixed attempt quota.

Report completed theory objectives, unresolved objectives and practical skills pending. State 'theory pathway completed' only if all critical objectives have applied/transferred evidence from the final cases. Never report 'qualified', 'certified' or 'safe to dive' from this course.

## Practical observation by a qualified instructor

Buoyancy/trim/propulsion; equipment checks and valve access; primary reel and jump/gap installation; marker identification including by touch; light/hand/touch communication; gas sharing while exiting; zero-visibility exit; lost-line, lost-diver and entanglement responses; and instrument familiarity require appropriate supervised practice and assessment. Chat cannot mark these passed.
