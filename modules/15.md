# 15 — Read the computer in context

status: draft
objective: INS-01

## Observable goal

Distinguish displayed ascent estimates from cave-exit obligations.

## Opening scene

Fictional classroom case. In the fictional case the Perdix 2 displays NDL 18 minutes and TTS 3 minutes. The team's model still estimates 12 minutes horizontal travel before open water. Leon calls TTS the time needed to get out of the cave.

## Exploration

A. Interpret the displayed ascent model.

B. Inspect the missing horizontal route and gas assumptions.

## Branch consequences

A: TTS models ascent and required stops; it does not include the horizontal cave return. NDL is not time remaining for cave penetration.

B: Route travel, team failures and donor gas need separate planning. A pressure/GTR display does not certify those assumptions.

## Mandatory explanation

Use knowledge/COMPUTER.md to explain depth, time, active gas, pressure, NDL, TTS, ceiling and CNS. Check actual mode and firmware before giving device instructions. The supplied recreational documentation cannot establish every technical-mode control. Defaults and update behaviour are not personal setting recommendations.

At the surface, identify the active gas, units, operating mode, pressure source, backup information and how the chosen fields support the plan. Practise instrument use in an appropriate controlled setting. If pressure data become unreliable, use the trained monitoring contingency and call the dive as appropriate; do not assume the last displayed value remains valid.

No button sequences for the secondary Uwatec model are invented here. No firmware number is labelled current merely because it appears in this course. Verify device-specific guidance before configuration changes.

## Worked application

The example's “12 minutes plus displayed TTS 3” highlights two obligations but is not a validated 15-minute total: the modelled TTS may change during the horizontal return. Recompute the planned profile and gas as appropriate; do not freeze a current display and extrapolate it as a guaranteed exit time.

## Misconception and feedback

Confusion: GTR or TTS automatically accounts for the cave route. Correction: establish the device model and separately account for horizontal exit.
