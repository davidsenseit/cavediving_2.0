# 16 — Build and challenge the team plan

status: draft
objective: INT-01

## Observable goal

Integrate limits, route, gas and practical boundaries.

## Opening scene

Fictional classroom case. Mira and Leon propose a fictional out-and-back route with one trained jump. Mira has accessible 24 L at 225 bar; Leon has accessible 20 L at 210 bar. The exercise specifies thirds, 5 bar increments, a known return route and personal exit-side markers. No real site or qualification is implied.

## Exploration

A. Build the initial allocation and route explanation.

B. Try to disprove the plan with a changed exit assumption.

## Branch consequences

A: The common gas budget is 1400 L, giving conservative thresholds 170 bar and 140 bar. A route explanation must preserve continuous owned line, personal references and the proven exit.

B: At thresholds, inventories are 4080 and 2800 L. A two-diver demand model can fail on the smaller donor even when matching arithmetic is correct.

## Mandatory explanation

A complete review separates authorization/competence, site conditions, equipment function, route, gas allocation, emergency demand, depth/gas/time limits, communication, entry/exit logistics and emergency support. Agree first-limit turn behaviour and how changed conditions cancel or shorten the plan. Verify actual pressures before entry; classroom starts are not measurements.

Work an emergency case explicitly. Given total emergency RMV 60 L/min, constant 15 m, 2-minute response and 12-minute travel, demand to the cave mouth is 2100 L. At the rounded thresholds the smaller donor nominally has 2800 L, leaving 700 L before ascent, residual and other omissions. With 18-minute travel instead, demand is 3000 L and that donor is short by 200 L. Thus the longer scenario fails even the incomplete model.

Mark what remains unresolved. The correct response to insufficient information is to identify it and obtain it, not silently fill in an optimistic value. Theory decisions and instructor-observed execution are different outputs.

## Worked application

Prepare a short brief: usable exit route; owned connections and marker convention; each start/turn pressure; why the first threshold turns the team; each donor's demand comparison; omitted ascent/residual; other earlier limits; communication and emergency support; practical skills needing assessment. Then attempt exercises/FINAL.md with different numbers.

## Misconception and feedback

Confusion: a correct spreadsheet proves the whole plan safe. Correction: arithmetic verifies only stated relationships, not site facts, competence or unmodelled failures.

## Memory callback

Retrieve continuity/direction, gas units, rounding and separate emergency demand without rereading worked answers first.
