# Roadmap

## Included in initial draft

- Self-contained agent instructions, onboarding and ordered course manifest.
- Sixteen taught modules, seven of them for progressive gas reasoning.
- Thirty-two module applications with rubrics and feedback, plus six integrated final cases.
- Consistent terminology, retrieval callbacks and accessible quantitative/route visuals.
- Equipment/preparation and instrument material with practical-training boundaries.
- Independent arithmetic fixtures, structural checks and CI workflow.

## Before treating subject content as verified

- Qualified subject review through the initial pull request, including gas-model assumptions, navigation conventions, emergency boundaries and physiology.
- Run fresh-conversation delivery tests in authenticated ChatGPT and Claude environments; verify private repository access and no-widget fallback.
- Observe whether questions are unambiguous with actual learners; adjust pacing and misconceptions from evidence.
- Conduct separate instructor-led practical assessment; the repository cannot perform this.

## Subsequent development

- Add further fresh calculation variants when review or learners reveal a gap.
- Assess retention after a later session before claiming improved retention.
- Add configuration-specific or site-specific learning only with an explicit scope and appropriate verification.
