# Instruments and the exit plan

status: draft

Depth, elapsed time, gas pressure, active breathing mix and decompression information must be interpreted together with the route and team plan. NDL is the model's remaining no-decompression time under its assumptions. It is not remaining cave time. TTS estimates ascent and required stops according to the computer model; it does not include swimming back through the cave. A ceiling is a decompression constraint, not the rock overhead. CNS % tracks modelled oxygen exposure; it is not a measurement of individual immunity to toxicity.

Gas Time Remaining (GTR) estimates depend on the device's gas/consumption assumptions and are not cave penetration permissions. A transmitter or battery failure does not mean gas vanished, but loss of reliable monitoring must be handled by the agreed trained contingency. Confirm actual pressure with the planned independent means; no unverifiable number can justify further penetration.

For a Perdix 2 familiarisation session, confirm the actual firmware, operating mode, units, gas analysis/active mix, time/depth, pressure source and relevant display fields at the surface. Test readability and agreed alerts before a demanding dive. Do not infer menu paths across modes or firmware. A recreational operating document does not supply every technical-mode procedure.

A version-specific example in the supplied material concerns firmware 102: fixed conservatism presets changed GF Low, while custom GF values were retained. This is an example of why an update warrants checking actual settings, not a recommendation of gradient factors. Do not label version 102 current/latest. Firmware-specific GPS receiving requires compatible equipment and surface conditions; it is not underwater cave positioning. Updates, battery work and configuration checks belong at the surface with the correct device documentation.

A backup computer is useful only when configured and understood for the plan. No Uwatec Smart 2 button sequence is specified here because model-specific operating instructions have not been established in this course.
